# Walkthrough Narrative

I approached the problem by first identifying the real bottleneck: the team does not simply need another dashboard; they need a faster and more consistent way to answer a recurring business question.

I chose the internal marketing analyst as the v1 user because analysts are the people currently doing this work manually. If the tool helps them produce answers faster and more consistently, the business impact is immediate. A client-facing portal can come later once the internal version is trusted.

The first version intentionally avoids advanced features such as AI chat, forecasting, and automated budget recommendations. Those features sound impressive, but they would increase scope before the team has validated the core workflow. The v1 goal is simple: show current performance across channels, highlight what changed, and point analysts toward the areas that need attention.

The main tradeoff is between ambition and reliability. A smaller, trusted snapshot is more useful than a broad tool that users cannot fully explain or verify.
